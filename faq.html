Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about custom denim manufacturing with KiKi Jeans.

About KiKi Jeans

What is KiKi Jeans?

KiKi Jeans is a professional custom denim clothing manufacturer based in XinTang, Guangzhou, China — the world's largest denim production hub. Established in 2012, we specialize in OEM and ODM denim manufacturing for international brands, e-commerce sellers, private label businesses, and influencers. Our factory covers 3,500 m², employs over 150 skilled workers, and produces more than 5 million pieces annually.

Where is KiKi Jeans factory located?

Our factory is located in XinTang, Guangzhou, China. XinTang is known as the "Denim Capital of the World," producing over 300 million pairs of jeans annually. Our location is 40 minutes from the Canton Fair exhibition hall and 30 minutes from Guangzhou airport, making factory visits easy and convenient for international clients.

How long has KiKi Jeans been in business?

KiKi Jeans has been manufacturing denim clothing since 2005, giving us over 20 years of OEM/ODM experience in the international garment industry.

What certifications does KiKi Jeans hold?

KiKi Jeans holds the following internationally recognized certifications: SGS (quality and safety testing), GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard), GRS (Global Recycled Standard), and OEKO-TEX Standard. These certifications confirm that our manufacturing processes and materials meet strict international quality, safety, and sustainability requirements.

Orders & Minimum Order Quantity

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our minimum order quantity is 100 pieces per style, per wash, with mixed sizes allowed. This low MOQ policy is designed to support startups, small brands, e-commerce sellers, and emerging denim labels who need professional manufacturing without committing to large volumes.

Can I order different styles together to meet the MOQ?

No. The MOQ of 100 pieces applies per style and per wash separately. For example, if you want 2 styles in 2 different washes, that would be 4 separate MOQ requirements (100 pcs each).

Is there a maximum order limit?

No. With an annual production capacity of over 5 million pieces and 150+ skilled workers, KiKi Jeans can handle both small brand orders and large-scale bulk production with stable delivery timelines.

What information do I need to place an order?

To get started, you can share your design ideas, reference photos, tech packs, or sketches. Our team will review your requirements, provide a price estimate, and guide you through the entire process from sample development to bulk production.

Products & Customization

What denim products can KiKi Jeans manufacture?

KiKi Jeans manufactures a full range of denim clothing, including: men's jeans, women's jeans, men's denim shorts, women's denim shorts, women's denim skirts, men's denim jackets, and women's denim jackets. All products support full customization in fit, wash, fabric, and branding.

What customization options are available?

We offer comprehensive customization including: custom cuts and fits (slim, regular, straight, bootcut, wide leg, etc.), custom denim washes (acid wash, stone wash, ripped, distressed, raw denim, etc.), custom brand labels (hang tags, woven labels, leather patches, metal buttons, rivets), custom packaging, and custom embroidery or printing.

What types of denim washes do you offer?

KiKi Jeans offers a wide range of denim washes, including: raw/dry denim, rinse wash, stone wash, acid wash, bleach wash, vintage wash, snow wash, tie-dye wash, distressed/ripped finish, and custom washes developed to match your brand's requirements.

Can I create my own private label jeans brand with KiKi Jeans?

Yes. KiKi Jeans provides a complete private label service. We can manufacture jeans under your brand name with fully customized labels, tags, buttons, and packaging. Many international clothing brands and e-commerce businesses have successfully launched their own denim lines through our private label program.

Do you offer OEM and ODM services?

Yes. We offer both OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturing) and ODM (Original Design Manufacturing) services. OEM means we produce jeans based on your existing designs. ODM means our design team creates new styles based on your brief, which you can then sell under your own brand.

Samples & Development

How long does sample production take?

Standard sample development takes 3 to 7 business days after we receive all required design information and confirm fabric and trim selections. Rush sample services may be available upon request.

How much does a sample cost?

Sample costs vary depending on the complexity of the design, fabric selection, and wash requirements. Please contact us directly for a sample quotation. Sample fees are typically deducted from the bulk order payment once production is confirmed.

Can I request changes to the sample before bulk production?

Yes. Sample revision is a standard part of our process. We will provide photos and videos of the sample for your review. You can request adjustments to fit, wash, details, or labels before approving the sample for bulk production.

Production & Quality Control

How does KiKi Jeans ensure product quality?

KiKi Jeans has an independent Quality Control (QC) department with dedicated QC and QA professionals. Every order goes through 3 stages of quality inspection: during fabric and material sourcing, during production, and before packing and shipment. We use over 200 sets of advanced cutting, sewing, and packing machines to maintain consistent quality.

Can I monitor the production progress remotely?

Yes. KiKi Jeans provides full production visualization. From sample stage to bulk production, we regularly send photos and can arrange video calls so you can visually check the progress of your order without visiting the factory.

What happens if I receive defective products?

KiKi Jeans stands behind the quality of every order. If you receive defective goods, please send us photos or video evidence. We will immediately arrange a full refund or remake the affected pieces at no additional cost.

Shipping & Logistics

Where does KiKi Jeans ship to?

We ship worldwide. Our factory's location near Guangzhou provides excellent access to major ports and logistics networks, allowing us to serve clients in the United States, Europe, Australia, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and beyond.

How long does bulk production and shipping take?

Bulk production time is typically 25 to 35 days depending on order volume and style complexity, after sample approval. Shipping time varies by destination and method (sea freight, air freight, or express courier).

Does KiKi Jeans assist with customs clearance?

Yes. Our professional foreign trade department provides assistance with export documentation, customs clearance, and shipping arrangements to help ensure smooth delivery to your destination.
