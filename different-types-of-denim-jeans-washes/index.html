Detail Description of Denim Washing

Garment Wash Denim

Garment wash is just for ordinary washing, it makes fabric cleaner, softer and more comfortable to
wear. lt takes only about 15-30 minutes for garment washing. lt鈥?s commonly used on colored apparels, such as t-shirt, hoodies, cargo pants, cotton pants and colored jackets, etc.

Stone Wash Denim

Stone washing can give raw denim texture and a rough effect. During stone washing process, the
water level should be low, just soaking the clothes; so that the stones can be in good contact with
the clothes. The washing time can be adjusted according to the different needs of customers to
achieve different washing effects.

Vintage washing is through various physical and chemical methods to make new jeans look as old

ACID Wash (Snow Wash)

Acid wash jeans are basically chemically treated to remove the color from the jeans, not from every.
