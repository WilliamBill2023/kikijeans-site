Who We Are

A Factory Partner for High-End Custom Jeans

Based in Zhongshan, Guangzhou, KiKi Jeans supports brands from concept development to boutique-ready production. Our team helps transform design references, fits, washes, labels and packaging ideas into practical denim products that can be sampled, refined and produced at scale.
